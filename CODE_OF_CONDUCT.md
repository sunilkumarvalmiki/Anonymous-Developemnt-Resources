# Code of Conduct

This project uses a simple standard for collaboration: be respectful, keep discussion focused on improving the resource catalog, and do not harass or abuse contributors.

## Expected Behavior

- Use welcoming and inclusive language.
- Give constructive feedback focused on the resource, category, or maintenance issue.
- Respect that maintainers may reject links that are stale, unsafe, illegal, promotional, or off-scope.
- Disclose conflicts of interest when submitting your own product, course, or service.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, insults, threats, or discriminatory language.
- Spam, repeated promotional submissions, or attempts to bypass review.
- Sharing pirated material, credential theft material, malware distribution links, or unsafe resources.
- Publishing private information without consent.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, reject, close, or remove contributions that violate this code of conduct or the resource-only scope of the repository.
